Model assumptions

  • Steady flow with no mass or momentum accumulation inside the control volume.
  • Incompressible fluid with constant density.
  • Uniform section-average velocity over each inlet and outlet jet.
  • Exposed inlet and outlet free-jet surfaces are at atmospheric pressure, so gauge-pressure terms there are zero.
  • The plate or vane is stationary and rigid.

Interpretation and limitation

For the active case, the fluid-on-plate reaction is Fx = 31.4159 N and Fy = 0 N, giving FR = 31.4159 N. Component signs use +x with the inlet jet and +y upward.

The velocity chart holds density, diameter, geometry, angles, split, and prescribed retention coefficient fixed while V changes.

This application uses a numerical control-volume momentum model. The flow visualization is illustrative and is not a full CFD simulation. It is not experimental evidence.
